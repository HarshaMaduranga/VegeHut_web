🍅 What We Do

VegeHut.lk is a web-based platform designed to connect farmers directly with large-scale buyers like wholesalers, retailers, and restaurants. We aim to reduce middleman exploitation and deliver fresh produce directly from the farm to the market.

🎯 Our Mission & Vision

Mission: To support Sri Lankan farmers by providing equal market access and improving income through direct buyer connections.

Vision: A transparent, efficient agricultural marketplace where freshness meets fairness.

🌈 Our Values

  • Fairness & Farmer Empowerment
  • Transparency in Trade
  • Tech-driven Agricultural Innovation
  • Sustainable Food Supply Chain
