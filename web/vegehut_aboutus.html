🍅 What We Do

VegeHut.lk is a revolutionary web-based platform designed to connect farmers directly with large-scale buyers like wholesalers, retailers, and restaurants. We aim to eliminate middleman exploitation and deliver fresh produce directly from the farm to the market.

🚀 Our Story

VegeHut.lk was born from our observation of the challenges faced by Sri Lankan farmers in accessing fair markets. As ICT students at the University of Sri Jayewardenepura, we recognized the power of technology to bridge the gap between farmers and buyers.

Our platform eliminates intermediaries, reduces costs, and ensures fresh produce reaches consumers faster while providing farmers with better income opportunities.
